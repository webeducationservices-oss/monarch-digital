Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do your restaurant consulting services cost?
Every engagement is scoped to your restaurant. After the walkthrough, you get a written plan with priorities, budget, and timeline, and you approve it before anything starts.
Do I have to use every service?
No. You can start with one service, like a training academy or a marketing refresh, and add more when the timing's right. Your plan keeps everything pointed in the same direction.
Can you work with the vendors I already have?
Yes. You can use our vendor network, or we'll coordinate the vendor relationships you already have. Either way, we handle the specs, timelines, and follow-ups so your team can stay focused on service.
Should I look for a restaurant consultant near me, or can you work remotely?
We're based in Tampa Bay, Florida, and work with restaurants nationwide. The first walkthrough can happen on site or virtually, and your plan sets how we'll work together from there.
How does your software work with my POS?
It runs alongside your existing POS, so the way your team rings in orders doesn't have to change. We can also coordinate POS onboarding and hardware timing, and build POS lessons into your training academy.
What do I need to provide?
Time for the walkthrough and access to what you already have: recipes, SOPs, your handbook, menus, brand files, and vendor contacts. We do the writing and building, and you review and approve each round.

Multi-Unit Restaurant Management

Multi-Unit Restaurant Management With One Standard

Growth is where standards slip. A new location picks its own scheduling app, trains its own way, and orders whatever uniforms the GM could find. Multi-unit restaurant management gets easier when every location runs on the same system from the day it opens.

Whether you're opening your second location or running a multi-unit restaurant group, we build that system once and roll it out: shared software, the same training academy, matching uniforms and furniture specs, marketing for every address, and reporting by location.

Guests feel it when standards hold. A regular who loves your first location expects the newest one to treat them the same way, and your team should be able to deliver that at any address.

One Login

One Login for Every Hire, at Every Location

Each hire gets one login for the operations app and the training academy. Access is set per person, so a GM sees their location and an operating partner sees the whole group. When someone leaves, offboarding cuts their access right away.

Schedules and training progress live in the same place across locations, which makes it simple to lend a trained server or a line lead to a new opening. Owners can compare locations each week and see which unit needs help and which one has a habit worth copying.

  • One login for operations and training
  • Access set by person, role, and location
  • Offboarding that cuts access immediately
  • A group-wide view for owners and partners
Multi-Location Marketing

Local SEO for Every Address

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a multi-location rollout cost?
It's scoped to your group, based on how many locations you have, which pieces you need, and the rollout order. You get a written plan with priorities, budget, and timeline, and you approve it before anything starts.
How long does a rollout take?
It depends on the number of locations and the scope. We build and prove the standard at one location first, then roll it out on the schedule in your plan, with new units opening from the playbook.
Can our locations keep the vendors they already use?
Yes, where they're working. We can coordinate your existing vendors or bring in our network, and we keep one spec so every location orders the same thing.
What do you need from us?
A list of locations and their leaders, your menus, handbook, and SOPs, your logo and brand files, and access to each location's Google Business Profile and website. We'll take it from there.
We only have one or two locations. Is this for us?
Yes. Two locations is a great time to set the standard, because every opening after that copies it. A single restaurant can start with one piece and add the rest as it grows.

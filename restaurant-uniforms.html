Uniform Programs

Uniforms Your Team Can Work a Double In

Restaurant uniforms are part of your guest's first impression and part of how your team feels at the end of a double. We build a uniform program around every role you staff, with the right fabric, fit, and logo for each, plus a simple way to outfit new hires and reorder.

We can run the program with your current supplier or source through our vendor network, and the same team can handle your restaurant furniture too. For hotel and resort food and beverage teams, we line up hotel uniforms across the restaurant, bar, banquets, and pool deck. We do the work. You guide the process.

Every Role

Restaurant Staff Uniforms Built Around the Job

Each station moves differently, so each role gets its own spec.

Servers and FOH

Polished pieces with pockets where servers need them for pens, wine keys, and order pads, in fabrics that stay sharp through a full section.

Kitchen and BOH

Chef coats, kitchen shirts, aprons, and pants in breathable fabric that handles heat, steam, and spills on the line and washes well every day.

Bar

Aprons, vests, or shirts that move with a bartender, keep sleeves out of the garnish tray, and hold up to citrus, syrup, and plenty of ice.

Host Stand

Hosts are the first people guests meet. Their uniforms set the tone for the room and stay comfortable through hours on their feet at the door.

Valet

Weather-ready layers, shoes made for running cars, and clear branding so guests know exactly who to hand their keys to.

Managers

A distinct look so guests and staff can find the manager on duty in a busy room, still built for long shifts on the floor.

Fabric and Function

Fabric That Handles Kitchen Heat and Spills

A uniform that looks great on day one and fades by month three costs you twice. We choose fabrics and finishes for how each station works: breathable weaves for the line, stain-resistant finishes for servers and bartenders, and weather-ready layers for valet.

Before you commit, your team wears samples through a real service. If something rides up, pulls, or soaks through, we fix the spec before the order goes in. Your dress code can live in your training academy as a day-one lesson, with a handbook e-signature to match.

  • Breathable, durable fabrics for kitchen heat
  • Stain-resistant finishes for spills and splashes
  • Colors and logos chosen to hold up to daily washing
  • Footwear and apron standards set by role
The Program Side

The details that keep a uniform program running long after launch day.

Logo and Embroidery

New-Hire Kits

Reorders and Replacements

Par levels by location, a simple reorder process for managers, and replacements for stained, torn, or worn-out pieces.

Rental vs. Purchase

Should You Rent or Buy Restaurant Uniforms?

Rental programs bundle laundering, repairs, and replacements into one recurring cost, which can suit chef coats and aprons that get washed every day. Buying gives you more control over style, branding, and fit, which often suits the front of house pieces guests see up close.

The right answer depends on your headcount, turnover, who does the laundry, and how often pieces get damaged, and many operators mix both. We'll run the numbers with you, put the trade-offs in writing, and recommend what fits. For groups, the same spec rolls out to every unit as part of a multi-location rollout.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a restaurant uniform program cost?
It's scoped to your restaurant, so we don't publish prices. Cost depends on your roles, headcount, pieces per person, logo work, and whether you rent or buy. Your written plan spells out the budget, and you approve it before anything is ordered.
How long does it take to roll out new uniforms?
It depends on whether pieces are in stock or made to order and how much logo work is involved. We plan backward from your launch or opening date and leave time for samples and fittings before the full order.
Can we keep our current uniform supplier?
Yes. We can run the program with your current supplier or bring options from our vendor network. Either way, you get one contact for sizing, orders, new-hire kits, and reorders.
What do you need from us?
Your list of roles and headcount, logo files and brand colors, the dress code from your handbook, and notes on what's working or failing today. We'll handle sizing and fittings with your team.
Do you work with single restaurants and multi-location groups?
Both. A single restaurant gets a program sized to its team. A group gets one spec per role, locked colors and logo placement, and a reorder process every GM can use, so every location looks like part of the same group.

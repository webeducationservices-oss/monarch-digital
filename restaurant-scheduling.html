Restaurant Scheduling Software

Restaurant scheduling software that starts from your book

Labor cost gets decided one shift at a time, on the schedule. Our restaurant scheduling software drafts each week by department from what you already know: the covers on the book, the staffing minimums for each station, and how similar weeks went. Your managers adjust the draft and publish.

Every draft follows your rules for availability, time off, hour caps, and split shifts. When it's published, each person gets their week by text, and the restaurant scheduling app handles swaps and time-off requests from there.

We set up your departments, schedulers, and rules, and an AI scheduling assistant can draft weeks and help cover call-outs, with a manager approving every change. We do the work. You guide the process.

Weekly Schedule

The whole week, by department, on one screen

Kitchen, dining room, bar, and any other department you run, each with a named scheduler. Coverage shows at a glance, and when the week looks right, the publish button sends it out.

Built-In Rules

Drafts that already know your rules

The first draft follows the rules your managers would otherwise check by hand.

Auto-drafted weeks

Each department's week is drafted from the book, your staffing minimums, and history, so the first version is close before anyone edits it.

Availability and time off

Drafts honor what each person can work and every time-off request you've approved.

Hour caps

Caps like 40 hours a week are respected, and part-timers who go over the line you set, such as 29 hours, get flagged.

No split shifts

Drafts can rule out split shifts, so nobody works lunch, waits around for three hours, and comes back for dinner.

Publish by text

Publishing texts each person their week. Later changes text only the people affected.

Swaps and time off

Staff request swaps, time off, and availability changes, and a manager approves each one.

Coverage and Hours

See coverage before the shift and hours after it

The coverage view shows where you're thin and where you're heavy, so you can fix Friday on Tuesday. The weekly hours report shows where the hours actually went.

On the night itself, a manager board at checkout makes it easy to cut staff and send people home when the dining room slows down.

  • A coverage view by department
  • A weekly hours report
  • A manager board for cuts at checkout
  • Call-out help from the scheduling assistant, approved by a manager
Labor Planning

Labor forecasting that keeps prime cost in view

Prime cost is your food and beverage cost plus labor, and many operators target a prime cost around 60 percent of sales. Labor is the part you set every week. These are the habits we build into how your schedule gets made.

  • Labor targets by department, set with you
  • Staffing minimums tied to covers on the book
  • Drafts that start from your own history
  • Overtime caught in the draft, before anyone works it
  • Part-time hours watched every week
  • Early cuts when the night slows
  • Weekly hours checked against your targets
  • Call-outs covered quickly, with a manager's OK

Case Study

Scheduling at a private dining club in Vail

For a private dining club in Vail, Colorado, we built scheduling for four departments: kitchen, dining room, bar, and valet. Each has named schedulers, and weeks are auto-drafted from the book, staffing minimums, and history.

Drafts respect availability, time off, and 40-hour caps, flag part-timers over 29 hours, and never split a shift. Publishing texts each person their week, and later changes text only the people affected. Swap, time-off, and availability requests go to a manager for approval, and managers get a coverage view and a weekly hours report. Each hire uses one login for the operations app, where scheduling lives, and the training academy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does restaurant scheduling software from Monarch cost?
It's scoped to your restaurant, based on your departments, locations, and what you want the system to do. You'll get a written plan with priorities, budget, and timeline, and nothing starts until you approve it.
How long does setup take?
Your plan sets the timeline. Setup means loading your team, departments, availability, and rules, then reviewing drafts with your managers until they match how you staff. If you're opening, we plan it around your hiring and training dates.
Does it work with my POS?
It runs alongside your POS. Your POS keeps handling orders and payments, and scheduling lives in the operations app we build for you, with one login per person.
What do I need to provide?
Your staff list with roles and availability, your departments and who schedules each one, staffing minimums, and any hour rules. A few weeks of past schedules help the first drafts start strong. We handle the setup.
Does it work for one restaurant and for multiple locations?
Yes. It works for a single restaurant or a group. Each location keeps its own departments and schedulers, every location follows the same rules, and a manager who moves between locations already knows the system.

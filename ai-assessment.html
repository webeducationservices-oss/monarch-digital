Frequently Asked Questions

Is the assessment really free?
Yes. There's no cost and no obligation. You get the evaluation on this page and by email, and Christy reaches out to talk it through if you'd like.
What does the AI look at?
Your answers, your public Google Business Profile (rating, review count, and recent reviews), and your public website (menu, ordering, reservations, mobile basics, and search signals). It doesn't log in anywhere or touch your POS.
How long does it take?
About three minutes to answer the questions. The AI usually finishes its evaluation in under a minute.
What happens with my information?
Christy receives your answers and your report so she can follow up. We don't sell or share your information.
Is the AI always right?
It's a fast, evidence-based first look, and every finding shows what it was based on. A person reviews it with you before anything is decided. A person always confirms anything that books, publishes, or sends.

Rather Talk It Through First?

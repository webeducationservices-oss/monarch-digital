Marketing That Fills Seats

Marketing That Starts With Your Covers

As a restaurant marketing agency, we start with your numbers: covers by daypart, check average, your slowest nights, and where new guests find you. Then every campaign gets a clear job, like filling Tuesdays, booking the private room, or bringing lunch regulars back for dinner.

We also build the software, training, and schedules restaurants run on, so campaigns and the operation get planned together. When a promotion is going to bring a rush, the kitchen, the floor, and the schedule know about it before the first ticket fires.

Websites and Site Migrations

A Website That Gets the Table Booked

Your website has a short list of jobs: show the menu, show the room, and make it easy to book, order, or call. We design and build restaurant websites that load fast on a phone, keep hours and menus easy to update, and put the reservation button where thumbs can find it.

Moving off an old platform is where restaurants can lose their search traffic, so we map every old URL to its new home first. For a private dining club in Vail, Colorado, we moved the public website off its old platform with 37 redirects and raised its SEO audit score from 66 to 98.

  • Menus that read well on a phone
  • Reservation, ordering, and gift card buttons in plain sight
  • Redirect maps so old links and rankings follow you
  • Separate landing pages for ads, events, and private dining

Services

What Our Restaurant Marketing Agency Handles

Everything you'd expect from a hospitality marketing agency, with each channel tied to covers, check average, or repeat visits.

Content Creation

Photos, short videos, and stories that show your food, your room, and the people behind both. We plan content around new dishes, events, and the seasons, then turn each round of photos into weeks of posts, emails, and ads.

Blogging

Useful posts about your menu, your neighborhood, private dining, and events, written around what local diners search for. Every post points readers to the pages that book tables.

Email Marketing and SMS

Event invites, new menu launches, birthday notes, and win-back messages for guests you haven't seen lately. Lists are sorted by interest and visit history, so regulars and first-timers each hear what fits.

Social Media

Restaurant social media marketing on the platforms your guests use. We plan the calendar, create the posts, and keep up with comments and messages, so questions about tonight's specials get answered.

Digital Advertising

Restaurant advertising on Google and social, aimed at your radius and your slow dayparts. Each campaign gets its own landing page, clear tracking, and a budget you approve.

SEO and Analysis

Restaurant SEO built on what diners search, like your cuisine plus your neighborhood. Each month you see rankings, clicks, calls, and bookings from search, explained in plain English.

Local Search and Reviews

Google Business Profile and Reviews, Kept Current

For a lot of guests, your Google Business Profile is the first look at your restaurant. We keep hours, holiday hours, menus, photos, and booking links accurate, post updates, and answer the questions people ask there.

Reviews get the same care. Each new review gets a drafted reply that a manager approves, service issues go straight to the GM, and happy guests get an easy way to share their night. We report ratings, search visibility, and campaign results together, so you can see what's moving.

  • Hours, holiday hours, and menus kept accurate
  • Fresh photos and posts for what's new
  • A drafted reply for every review, approved by a manager
  • Service issues flagged to the GM

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does restaurant marketing cost with Monarch?
Engagements are scoped to each restaurant, so there's no set price list. After a walkthrough, you get a written plan with priorities, budget, and timeline, and nothing starts until you approve it. If paid ads are part of the plan, you set the ad budget.
How long before we see results?
Some pieces move fast. For a private dining club in Vail, Colorado, the public website was live within the first month. Ads and email start reaching guests the day they launch, while SEO and reviews build over months, so your plan pairs quick wins with steady work.
Can you work with our current website, photographer, or reservation platform?
Yes. We coordinate with the vendors and tools you already have, or bring in our own network when something isn't working. If we move your website, we map redirects first so your search traffic follows you.
What do you need from us to get started?
Access to your website, Google Business Profile, social accounts, and ad accounts, plus your menu, photos, and recent cover counts by day. Most of all, we need someone on your team to approve content. We do the work. You guide the process.
Do you work with single restaurants or multi-location groups?
Both. An independent restaurant gets a plan sized to one dining room. A group gets one brand standard with local SEO, a Google Business Profile, and a location page for every address, plus reporting by location.
Will AI be writing our posts?
AI helps draft captions, emails, review replies, and ad copy so the work moves faster. A person reviews everything, and nothing publishes or sends until it's approved.

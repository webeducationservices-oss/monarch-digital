Frequently Asked Questions

How much does restaurant consulting with Monarch cost?
Every engagement is scoped to your restaurant. After the walkthrough, you get a written plan with priorities, budget, and timeline, and nothing starts until you approve it. The free AI assessment is a good first look at your gaps.
How long does a project take?
It depends on what we're building, and your written plan sets the timeline before work starts. For scale, our private dining club project in Vail went from first line of code to opening in about seven months.
How does your software fit with my POS?
It runs alongside your existing POS, so your team keeps ringing in orders the way they do today. On bigger projects we can also coordinate POS onboarding and hardware timing, and build POS lessons into your training academy.
What do I need to provide?
Time for the walkthrough, plus what you already have: recipes, SOPs, your handbook, menus, brand files, and vendor contacts. We write and build from there, and you review and approve each piece.
Do you work with single restaurants or only groups?
Both. We work with single independent restaurants and large multi-location projects. The process stays the same, and the plan scales to fit.
Is Monarch a restaurant management company?
We're a consulting and build partner. You and your team keep running service and making the calls, while we build and set up the systems behind it: software, training, marketing, scheduling, and sourcing. Then we stay on for support.

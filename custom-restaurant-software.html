Restaurant App Development

Restaurant app development that starts on your floor

Our restaurant app development begins with your service. We walk through a full night with you, from the first reservation call to the last check. Then we build the screens your people need, including guest and member apps, staff portals, and admin tools.

Your custom restaurant software works alongside your POS. The POS keeps ringing in orders and taking payments, and the tools we build cover everything around it: who's coming tonight, where they'll sit, what they're allergic to, and how the kitchen is pacing.

We do the work. You guide the process. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

Host Stand and Waitlist

A host stand that sees the whole night

Your host works the hardest spot in the building on a Friday night. We give them a live floor map with table status, a waitlist that quotes real waits, and ranked seat suggestions, so the next party gets the right table without a huddle at the stand.

  • Tables that combine, plus a slider to preview later tonight
  • Live wait quotes and one-tap table-ready texts for walk-ins
  • Returning guests recognized by phone number
  • Server sections, rest time between seatings, and caps on covers per 15 minutes
  • A daily report on how accurate the wait quotes were
Reservations and Guest Apps

Reservations and guest apps that give every request a clear answer

Guests and members book on the web or on their phone. Full time slots show as unavailable, and every request ends somewhere clear: confirmed, offered an alternative, waitlisted, or held for your team to review.

  • Booking windows that can differ by membership tier or guest type
  • An automatic waitlist that offers a freed-up table to the next party, then moves down the line
  • Reminder emails on your schedule, never overnight, in wording your managers can edit
  • Event RSVPs, messages to your team, and service requests in the same app
Server Tablets

Every server walks up already knowing the table

The moment a party is seated, the server's tablet shows a brief with allergies first, then preferences, birthdays, and recent visits. Notes taken at the table save to the guest's file, so the next server knows too.

  • A guest memory file with allergies, likes, birthdays, visits, and server notes, rebuilt every night
  • A shift plan that assigns the day's bookings to tables, with pre-shift, mid-shift, and checkout views and a printed day sheet
  • Server checkout with side work, plus a manager board for cutting staff and sending people home
Kitchen Display

A kitchen screen that shows BOH the pace of the room

A clock runs on every seated table, allergies show at the table, arrivals due are on screen, and a pacing line shows expo how the night is tracking. There's a full-screen display mode, and behind it sit pacing analysis, an allergen-filtered menu, and station prep sheets.

Staff Portals and Admin Tools

The back office your managers keep asking for

The pieces that keep a restaurant running between services, built into the same system.

Staff portals

One login per hire for the operations app and the training academy. Offboarding cuts access immediately.

Admin tools

A careers pipeline with an electronic job application, house rules for turn times by season and party size, message templates, per-user access, and audit logs.

Texting

Table-ready texts for walk-ins, and each person's schedule texted when a manager publishes the week.

Outside services

Connections to the services you rely on. At a private dining club in Vail, Colorado, rides book straight into the local car service's dispatch system with live status.

Reporting

Turn times, wait-quote accuracy, a nightly service rollup, scheduled email reports, and an audit email for every booking.

Practice mode

A full practice simulator with sample guests, so new staff learn the system without touching a real reservation.

Case Study

About seven months, from first line of code to opening

For a private dining club in Vail, Colorado, we wrote the first line of code in April 2026 and built everything for the club's November 2026 opening. The public website was live within the first month.

Along the way we built a member app for the web and mobile, reservations with booking windows by membership tier, an automatic waitlist, a host stand with a live map of 38 tables, server tablets, a kitchen screen, scheduling, a training academy, and an admin portal. That's 102 app screens across six web properties, and the operations tools run alongside the club's POS.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does custom restaurant software cost?
Every engagement is scoped to your restaurant, so there's no price list. After a walkthrough, you get a written plan with priorities, budget, and timeline, and nothing starts until you approve it. You can begin with the piece that costs your team the most time, like the host stand or the waitlist, and add from there.
How long does a custom build take?
It depends on scope, and your written plan sets the timeline before work begins. For a private dining club in Vail, Colorado, we went from the first line of code to opening in about seven months, with the public website live in the first month.
Will it work with my POS?
Yes. Our software works alongside your POS. The POS keeps handling orders and payments, and the tools we build handle reservations, the host stand, guest files, pacing, and staff. If you're opening, we can also coordinate POS onboarding and hardware timing so everything is ready together.
Can you build a restaurant online ordering system?
We scope it like everything else. Many POS systems already include online ordering, and if yours does the job, we'll keep it and make sure your website and guest app send people there. If you need something it can't do, we'll plan it with you.
What do I need to provide?
Mostly your time and know-how. We'll ask for a walkthrough, your floor plan, house rules like turn times and party sizes, and a manager who can answer questions during the build. We do the work. You guide the process.
Do you work with single restaurants or groups?
Both. We build for a single independent restaurant or a large multi-location group. For groups, every location runs the same system, so a host who picks up a shift across town already knows it.

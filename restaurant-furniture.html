Floor Plan and Seating Mix

Table Configuration Built for Covers and Turns

Every floor plan is a set of trade-offs. Booths are comfortable, and guests settle in. Two-tops that push together let the host seat a party of six without holding a whole section. Bar seats catch walk-ins and solo diners.

We work from your floor plan to find the mix that fits your concept and your volume, with aisles wide enough for servers carrying full trays and a clear path from expo to every table.

The mix also sets your pace. Flexible two-tops and four-tops help a high-volume room keep turning, and booths and lounge seating suit a longer, slower dinner. We match the mix to the night you want to run and the covers you need.

  • Seat count and table mix by section
  • Two-tops and four-tops that combine for larger parties
  • Server aisles, bus stations, and the path from the kitchen
  • Bar, lounge, private dining, and patio zones

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does restaurant furniture cost through Monarch?
Every project is scoped to your restaurant, so we don't publish prices. Your written plan includes a budget by category, like seating, tables, booths, patio, and custom pieces, and you approve it before anything is ordered.
How long does it take to get restaurant furniture?
Lead times vary by piece, maker, and finish, and custom pieces take longer than in-stock seating. We build the schedule backward from your opening date and order the long-lead pieces first, so furniture lands when the space is ready for it.
Can you work with our designer and the vendors we already use?
Yes. We work alongside your designer, architect, and builders. You can source through our vendor network, or we can coordinate the vendors you already have, with one point of contact for samples, orders, freight, and install timing.
What do you need from us to get started?
A floor plan or drawings, your design direction or your designer's spec, a target seat count, your opening date, and a budget range. For a remodel, photos of the current room help. We'll walk the space with you, on site or virtual.
Do you furnish single restaurants and multi-location groups?
Both. For a group, we keep one furniture spec with the same finishes and fabrics across locations, so a new unit matches the rest and a damaged chair can be replaced to match.
Do you handle delivery and installation?
We coordinate freight, receiving, and install timing with your builders and installers. Deliveries get checked at receiving, so damaged or wrong pieces get caught before they reach the dining room.
